FAQ

Common Questions

What areas does Busy Bee serve?

We are based in Northborough, MA and serve Worcester County, Middlesex County and Norfolk County — including Worcester, Shrewsbury, Westborough, Marlborough, Framingham, Hudson, Leominster, Fitchburg, Holden, Milford and many more towns.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Estimates are always free with no pressure and no obligation. Call, text or fill out our online form and we'll get back to you quickly.

Are you insured?

Yes. Busy Bee is fully insured for residential and commercial work. Certificates of insurance are available on request.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. We don't require contracts. Many customers choose recurring service because it's convenient, but you can book one-time cleanings anytime.

What if I'm not happy with the results?

Your satisfaction is 100% guaranteed. If something isn't right, let us know and we'll come back and make it right at no cost.
